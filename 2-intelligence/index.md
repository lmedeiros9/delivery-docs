# 2 | Intelligence

**Discovery, risk, and account intel.** What we learn before and during a project that keeps it on track.

## What lives here

- **Discovery to delivery hand-off:** spotting hidden alignment gaps and technical debt before the build starts.
- **Risk:** catching requirements and scope creep that put a project at risk, early.
- **Account health:** watching for sponsor changes and shifts in client sentiment.

## Weekly rhythm

Run a weekly risk review that looks forward, not back. Treat it as active threat-hunting. Ask: which project has an assumption that could break during integration testing?
