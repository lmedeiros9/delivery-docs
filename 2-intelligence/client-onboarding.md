# Client Onboarding

- **Purpose:** Set every new client engagement up the same way, so nothing important gets missed at the start.
- **Owner:** Director of Delivery
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-12
- **Applies to:** Client Leads and the delivery crew

## Pre-engagement checklist

Before work starts, confirm each of these:

- Legal and contract review
- Access provisioning (Salesforce, email, docs)
- Kickoff scheduled
- Stakeholders identified
- Communication preferences agreed
- Success criteria aligned with the client
