# Definition of Done

- **Purpose:** Give the whole crew one shared answer to "is this finished?"
- **Owner:** Director of Delivery
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-12
- **Applies to:** Everyone on the delivery crew

## Work is done when

1. It meets the requirements agreed with the client.
2. A checker has verified all five quality pillars, or review was skipped for low-risk work and the reason is documented. See [Maker-Checker]({{ site.baseurl }}/3-operations/maker-checker.html).
3. Documentation is current, including assumptions, decisions, and notes for whoever supports it next.
4. The Client Lead has what they need to deliver.

> The Client Lead owns delivery to the client. Make sure they know the work is ready.
