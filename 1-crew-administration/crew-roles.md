# Crew Roles

- **Purpose:** Make it clear who owns what on a client account.
- **Owner:** Director of Delivery
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-12
- **Applies to:** Everyone on the delivery crew

## Who does what

| Role | What they own |
|------|---------------|
| **Client Lead** | The client lifecycle and the project, start to finish: status, priorities, and client communication. |
| **Business Analyst** | Client check-ins, reporting, and requirements. |
| **Admin** | Org audits, flow reviews, and Maker-Checker reviews. |
| **Tech crew** | Maintenance, data quality, and deployments. |
| **Director of Delivery** | The Delivery Playbook, quality standards, capacity planning, and the escalation point for the crew. |

Anyone on the crew can be the maker on one piece of work and the checker on another. See [Maker-Checker]({{ site.baseurl }}/3-operations/maker-checker.html).
