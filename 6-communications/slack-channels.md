# Slack Channels

- **Purpose:** Explain what each Delivery channel is for.
- **Owner:** Director of Delivery
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-12
- **Applies to:** Everyone on the delivery crew

| Channel | What it's for |
|---------|---------------|
| **#delivery** | The main team channel |
| **#ready-for-takeoff** | New requests ready for a crew member to claim |
| **#ready-for-check** | Work waiting for a checker |
| **#delivery-escalations** | Urgent issues that need the Director of Delivery |

See [Maker-Checker]({{ site.baseurl }}/3-operations/maker-checker.html) for how the two "ready" channels fit into the workflow.
