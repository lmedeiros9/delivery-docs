# Capacity Planning

- **Purpose:** Show who is working on what, and where the crew has room or is stretched.
- **Owner:** Director of Delivery
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-12
- **Applies to:** Delivery crew and Client Leads

## What we track

- **Crew:** certifications, current utilization, and available hours.
- **Clients:** active engagements, project stage, expected duration, and resource needs.
- **Assignments:** who is on what, hours allocated, conflicts, and burn-down.

## How often it updates

The capacity planner refreshes weekly, on Mondays.

## The three views

- A weekly capacity snapshot
- Monthly utilization by crew member
- A three-month client forecast

Leadership uses these views to see when crew capacity can't cover client demand, which feeds staffing decisions.
