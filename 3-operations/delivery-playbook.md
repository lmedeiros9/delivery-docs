# The Delivery Playbook

- **Purpose:** Explain what the Delivery Playbook is and where to find the full version.
- **Owner:** Director of Delivery
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-12
- **Applies to:** Delivery crew, Client Leads, and leadership

## What it is

The Playbook is the operational north star for Delivery. It documents how we govern work, manage quality, plan capacity, onboard clients, and escalate decisions.

- It is the source of truth for delivery operations and decision rights.
- It is a living document that changes as our processes evolve.
- It is a reference hub for the crew, leadership, and stakeholders.

## What it is not

- A rigid rulebook. Processes change, and so does the Playbook.
- Job descriptions. Role details live in the individual role descriptions.
- A replacement for the Slack channels the crew already works in.

## What Delivery owns

- **Governance:** decision frameworks, approval gates, and escalation paths.
- **Quality assurance:** the Maker-Checker workflow, the five quality pillars, and delivery standards.
- **Capacity planning:** crew utilization, client load, and assignments.
- **Portfolio oversight:** active engagements, staffing alignment, and delivery health.
