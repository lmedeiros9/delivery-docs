# Delivery Models

- **Purpose:** Describe the three ways C2 delivers work.
- **Owner:** Director of Delivery
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-12
- **Applies to:** Delivery crew and Client Leads

## The three models

| Model | Focus | Typical duration |
|-------|-------|------------------|
| **Projects** | Defined scope and discrete deliverables | 4 to 12 weeks |
| **Ongoing Support (OS)** | Monthly retainer, steady state | Ongoing |
| **Managed Services (MS)** | Full platform stewardship and strategy | Ongoing |
