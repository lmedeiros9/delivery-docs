# Monthly Rhythm

- **Purpose:** Give the crew a shared monthly cadence, with protected time for maintenance.
- **Owner:** Director of Delivery
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-12
- **Applies to:** Delivery crew and Client Leads

## The month at a glance

| When | What happens |
|------|--------------|
| **Day 1** | Planning kickoff, 15 to 20 minutes. The crew aligns on priorities, capacity, and maintenance concerns. |
| **Days 10 to 12** | Mid-month maintenance window. This time is protected. |
| **Days 20 to 25** | Late-month deliverables. |
| **Last 3 days** | Admin wrap-up and hours submission. |

## Why the maintenance window exists

Maintenance is the first thing to slip when a month gets busy, and it tends to pile up at the end. A locked window in the middle of the month gives it a structural home, so it doesn't depend on anyone finding spare time.

## Keeping it visible

Calendar reminders and a shared calendar keep each phase on everyone's radar.
