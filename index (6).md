## How this site is organized

Every section has a number and a name. The number never changes, so "that's a 3-series question" always means Operations.

| # | Section | What lives here |
|---|---------|-----------------|
| 1 | Administration | Capacity, skills, onboarding, performance |
| 2 | Intelligence | Discovery, risk, account health |
| 3 | Operations | The playbook, quality checks, Definition of Done |
| 4 | Logistics | Reusable assets, templates, provisioning, deployment tooling |
| 5 | Plans | Roadmaps, release alignment, scope and SOWs |
| 6 | Communications | Status standards, internal comms, documentation |
