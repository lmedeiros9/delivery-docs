# Delivery Docs

How the crew delivers. One place to find the process, the standards, and the templates.

## Start here

- **New to the crew?** Begin with [1 | Crew]({{ site.baseurl }}/1-crew/).
- **Starting a project?** Go to [2 | Intake & Risk]({{ site.baseurl }}/2-intake-risk/), then [5 | Plans]({{ site.baseurl }}/5-plans/).
- **Building?** See [3 | Operations]({{ site.baseurl }}/3-operations/) and [4 | Resources]({{ site.baseurl }}/4-resources/).
- **Talking to a client?** Check [6 | Comms & Systems]({{ site.baseurl }}/6-comms-systems/).

## How this site is organized

Every section has a number and a plain name. The number never changes, so "that's a 3-series question" always means Operations.

| # | Section | What lives here |
|---|---------|-----------------|
| 1 | Crew | Onboarding, roles, quals, capacity planning |
| 2 | Intake & Risk | Discovery, permissions, data handling |
| 3 | Operations | The playbook, delivery cycles, quality checks, Definition of Done |
| 4 | Resources | Environments, templates, tooling |
| 5 | Plans | Scoping, estimating, roadmaps, renewals |
| 6 | Comms & Systems | Client communication, status reporting, documentation standards |
