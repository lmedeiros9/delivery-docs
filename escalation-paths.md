# Escalation Paths

- **Purpose:** Show who to bring an issue to, and when.
- **Owner:** Director of Delivery
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-12
- **Applies to:** Everyone on the delivery crew

## Escalate to the Director of Delivery

- A client request outside the defined scope
- A quality gate failure on strategic work
- Crew availability or burnout risk
- Budget or timeline misalignment
- A relationship or communication breakdown

## Escalate to the ELC

- Client relationship risk or an executive-level issue
- Pricing or contract changes
- Major scope creep
- Staffing constraints
- Strategic questions about the delivery model

## Flag, with no decision needed

- Weekly crew utilization
- Upcoming resource constraints
- Client satisfaction signals
- Delivery health numbers

## During a Maker-Checker review

- **A checker finds a blocker:** tell the Director of Delivery right away and post in the escalations channel. Say what the blocker is and what needs to happen next.
- **A decision is ambiguous or split:** talk it over with a peer or the Director of Delivery, and write down the reasoning.
- **Client approval is needed to proceed:** bring in the Client Lead and leadership.
- **The review threatens a client deadline:** flag it immediately. The options are expedited approval, a phased rollout, or a timeline change.

> These rules are still being captured as patterns emerge. If you hit a situation that isn't covered here, escalate anyway and tell the Director of Delivery so this page can grow.
